# Prose craft Resources

## Knowledge

- Book: _Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace_ by Joseph M. Williams (with Joseph Bizup in later editions)
  The core text. First published 1981 as _Style: Ten Lessons in Clarity and Grace_; now in its
  [13th edition from Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/style-lessons-in-clarity-and-grace/P200000002140/9780137536603).
  Any recent edition is fine — later ones reorder the lessons and push further into document-level structure.
  A shorter cut exists as _Style: The Basics of Clarity and Grace_.
  Use for: characters as subjects, actions as verbs, cohesion and coherence, emphasis, concision.
  ([Wikipedia on the editions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Style:_Lessons_in_Clarity_and_Grace))
- [Gopen & Swan, "The Science of Scientific Writing" (_American Scientist_ 78:6, 1990, pp. 550–558)](https://www.americanscientist.org/blog/the-long-view/the-science-of-scientific-writing)
  Twelve pages that carry much of Williams' cargo, free. Reader-expectation theory: subject–verb
  proximity, the stress position at the end of a sentence, old information before new.
  Use for: the fastest possible introduction, and as the primary source for the first lesson.
  A [PDF copy is mirrored by USENIX](https://www.usenix.org/sites/default/files/gopen_and_swan_science_of_scientific_writing.pdf).
- [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) by Daniele Procida
  A systematic framework splitting documentation into four modes — tutorials, how-to guides, technical
  reference, explanation — and covering content, style, and architecture. Developed at Divio (2014–2021),
  now maintained standalone. ([Colophon](https://diataxis.fr/colophon/))
  Use for: READMEs, product docs, and any piece where the real problem is that two modes are fused.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Write the Docs](https://www.writethedocs.org/) and its Slack
  Where the Diátaxis discussion actually happens, per the project's own colophon.
  Use for: critique of a docs structure, and how practitioners handle the mode boundaries.
- [r/technicalwriting](https://www.reddit.com/r/technicalwriting/)
  Shared with [[asd-ste100]]. Use for: second opinions on a rewrite.

## Gaps

- No community identified yet for general prose craft at this level that is not a creative-writing group.
  Look for an editing-focused one before the first session.
