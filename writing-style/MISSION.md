# Mission: Prose craft (Williams, then Diátaxis)

> **Status: queued, not started.** This mission is provisional — written from what the STE sessions
> revealed, not from an interview. Confirm or rewrite it at the first session.

## Why
Nitan's blog posts, product writing, and posts for engineers are already clean at the sentence level.
What varies in quality is what happens *between* sentences: flow, emphasis, and the order ideas arrive in.
[[asd-ste100]] taught him what to remove; this mission is about what to build. The goal is prose that
reads as inevitable rather than assembled, without flattening his voice.

## Success looks like
- He can diagnose why a paragraph drags, in Williams' terms: nominalised verbs, characters missing from subjects, new information landing where old information belongs.
- He can revise for cohesion (old-before-new) and emphasis (the point in the stress position) as a deliberate pass, not by ear.
- He can tell which of the four Diátaxis modes a piece of documentation is in, and split a README section that is secretly two modes fused.
- He keeps the rhythm and dryness of his own voice through all of it.

## Constraints
- Start only when the [[asd-ste100]] core is done (roughly lessons 1–5 there). Two writing habits at once means neither sticks.
- Short sessions, one win each — same shape as the STE workspace.
- Works best on his real drafts. Prefer a live post over invented examples.

## Out of scope
- Fiction, poetry, and narrative craft.
- SEO, growth, and audience-building advice.
- Grammar-as-correctness (comma rules, split infinitives). Williams is explicit that these are folklore; this mission follows him.
