# Mission is general writing, not aerospace documentation

Nitan starts from zero on ASD-STE100 (knows the name only) and wants it for his personal blog,
product writing, and social posts for an engineering audience — not for maintenance manuals or
S1000D work. This reframes the whole course: STE is a diagnostic and a rewriting discipline here,
and every lesson must separate rules that transfer to voice-driven prose from rules that only make
sense inside a procedure.

**Implications:** teach with his own text wherever possible; never present full compliance as the
goal for a blog post; reconcile each rule against his documented writing voice (see `NOTES.md`).
He asked for all four modes — rule-by-rule teaching, rewrite drills, his real text, and printable
reference sheets — so lessons should carry a drill and a reference document should trail each cluster of rules.
