# ASD-STE100 Resources

## Knowledge

- [ASD-STE100 Issue 9, the standard itself (PDF, free)](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf)
  The primary source: Part 1 is the 53 writing rules in 9 sections, Part 2 is the ~900-word
  dictionary. Issue 9 (15 Jan 2025) re-titled the standard "Standard for Technical Documentation"
  and reworded 31 of the 53 rules. Use for: the exact text of any rule, and the approved
  part-of-speech/meaning of any word. Note: the PDF is encrypted, so it does not machine-extract —
  read it in a PDF viewer.
- [ASD-STE100 — About STE](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html)
  Official overview: history from AECMA Simplified English (1986), the two-part structure,
  and the stated purpose (reduce Human Factor risk in maintenance). Use for: framing and quotable definitions.
- [ASD-STE100 — FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html)
  Official answers on non-aerospace use ("its benefits are indeed applicable across all sectors"),
  cost (free), and how Technical Names / Technical Verbs let you legally use words outside the dictionary.
  Use for: every "am I allowed to…" question.
- [ASD Europe: Issue 9 release note](https://www.asd-europe.org/news-media/news-events/news/simplified-technical-english-asd-ste100-issue-9/)
  What changed in Issue 9, including the 555 updated dictionary entries and the ISO 1087-1:2019
  alignment that introduced "technical nouns" and "technical verbs". Use for: version questions.
- [Wikipedia: Simplified Technical English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Technical_English)
  Fast, accurate summary of the headline numeric rules (20/25-word limits, 6-sentence paragraphs,
  3-word noun clusters). Use for: orientation only — always confirm against the PDF before quoting.
- [tcworld: ASD-STE100 Issue 9 — setting a standard for technical documentation](https://www.tcworld.info/e-magazine/technical-writing/asd-ste100-issue-9-setting-a-standard-for-technical-documentation)
  Practitioner-facing walkthrough of Issue 9 from the technical-communication trade press.
  Use for: how working technical writers actually adopt it.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/technicalwriting](https://www.reddit.com/r/technicalwriting/)
  Active, practitioner-heavy. Use for: "is STE worth it for X", tool recommendations, sanity checks on rewrites.
- [tekom / tcworld community](https://www.technical-communication.org/)
  The European technical-communication association behind tcworld. Use for: standards-level discussion, events.
- [STE Maintenance Group, via the official contact form](https://www.asd-ste100.org/contact.html)
  The people who write the standard. They answer interpretation questions and take change requests.
  Use for: a genuine ambiguity in a rule — the authoritative last resort.
